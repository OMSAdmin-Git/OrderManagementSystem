## 📋 1. Testing Overview & Objective

The objective of Stage 2 testing is to verify that staged Suzuki records (`SUZUKI_SPIRITS_*`) generated by Stage 1 Pre-Import are correctly processed through the complete 8-step registration and reconciliation pipeline:

1. **ORDERS_STAGE Cleaning**: Uncommitted records for customer `5455` and folder type `4` are purged.
2. **Data Extraction & Transform**: Records are extracted from `SUZUKI_SPIRITS_*` and converted into `ORDERS_STAGE` rows with working calendar due dates (`ADD_WORKING_DAYS2`).
3. **Profit Center Resolution**: Part numbers are checked in `USRDEFFLDF` (`ITEMM` / `FRECKEY = PRODUCT_CODE`) to resolve the child `CUSTOMER_SETTING_ID`.
4. **Special Code Adjustments**:
   - `0630`: Increments demand quantity of matching `0600`.
   - `663N`: Updates due date of matching `6624`.
   - `663S`: Updates demand quantity of matching `6624`.
5. **Stage Validation**: Checks required fields and ensures Profit Centers are resolved before commit.
6. **Reconciliation (`UPDATE ②`)**:
   - Destination conversion via `SUZUKI_SPIRITS_CONVERSION_MST`.
   - Forecast wash replacement (`ReplaceNaijiRelationSuzuki` — `0602` skipped; `0600`, `0813`, `6604` washed).
   - Forecast consumption (`ReconcileForecastSuzuki` — `0650/0651` consumes `0600`, `0814` consumes `0813`, `6624` consumes `6604`).
7. **Master Commitment**: Valid records committed to `ORDERS` and `ORDER_HISTORY`; staging records updated to `STATUS = 'REGISTERED'`.
8. **STRA Integration**: Automatic lead time calculation (`STRA納期設定`) updating orders to `STATUS = 'DUE_SET'`.
